What phase 1 built:

- **Envelope** (`src/components/opening/Envelope.astro`, `src/scripts/opening.ts`, `src/lib/opening.ts`):
  - A navy envelope with a gold ogee lattice, the AA wax seal and "Tap to open".
  - On a tap: the seal lifts, the flap folds back in 3D, the letter rises with a glow and particles, comes forward, then a cream "sheet" grows from the letter to fill the screen and hands over to the hero.
  - Return visits open by themselves (2 s, silent). Reduced motion gets a fade. `#section` links land on their section afterwards. Without JavaScript there's no envelope at all.
- **Site-wide:** the Sayed Builders palette (cream, gold, navy) and the Heirloom fonts (Luxurious Script, Marcellus, Cormorant Garamond). The arch and the old painted florals are hidden. The hero was rebuilt as "the letter".
- **Music slot:** `public/assets/audio/opening.mp3`, with a speaker button (`MusicToggle.astro`, `src/scripts/music.ts`). Music starts only on a tapped opening.

## The new direction (what the user wants next)

> "I actually want the envelope to open and a light shining that portals the user to a whole different view with background video play and then things animating in."

So the opening should become:

1. The envelope opens (the current coded envelope can stay).
2. **A burst of light** from the envelope fills the screen, like the pink reference (zareqia "royal-prestige"), where a starburst of light comes out of the opened envelope.
3. **A portal into a different scene:** a full-screen background **video** playing behind the content, like the reference's terrace with blowing curtains.
4. **The content animates in** over that video.

This replaces the phase 1 handoff, where the letter grows into a cream page. It probably also changes the hero (cream "letter" today; content over video tomorrow).

## Open questions for the next conversation

- What should the video scene be? The user generates it, so it needs a brief: subject, palette (Sayed navy, gold and cream), loop length, size budget, portrait vs landscape, and what to show on laptops.
- Does the video play behind only the first screen (the hero), or behind the whole site?
- Do the letter and its rise stay before the light burst, or does the light come straight out of the opened flap?
- What do guests see before the video has loaded on slow data (a poster frame)?
- Reduced motion and return visits: still a fade and an auto-open?
- Does the music still start on the tap?
